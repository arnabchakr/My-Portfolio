About me

I’m Arnab, a rock singer, musician and music producer. For the past 4 years I’ve been playing live in cafés, pubs and universities, working with bands, and releasing my own music.

Whether it’s a packed college fest or a cozy café night, I bring the same energy: real sound, real emotion, and a set that keeps the room with me.
